# Sheshaan Global SEO Strategy

## Commercial Search Intent

| Target page                 | Primary intent                      | Example keyword themes                                                        | Page purpose                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/`                         | Brand and broad exporter discovery  | Indian agricultural products exporter, agri commodity exporter India          | Explain what Sheshaan Global supplies, who it serves and how buyers can inquire.     |
| `/products`                 | Product catalogue discovery         | agricultural export product list, HS code export catalogue                    | Help buyers browse export lines and reach dedicated product pages.                   |
| `/products/[slug]`          | Product-specific commercial inquiry | product exporter India, product supplier India, bulk product supplier         | Convert qualified buyers with product, packing, standards, documents and quote CTAs. |
| `/contact`                  | RFQ submission                      | request export quote, agricultural export inquiry                             | Capture complete B2B inquiry data and provide WhatsApp/email fallback.               |
| `/about`                    | Entity and trust research           | Sheshaan Global exporter, Indian sourcing partner                             | Clarify business positioning without unsupported claims.                             |
| `/export-process`           | Buyer education before inquiry      | how agricultural products are exported from India                             | Explain the transaction workflow from RFQ to shipment follow-up.                     |
| `/quality`                  | Risk reduction                      | agricultural export quality checks India                                      | Show how sourcing, grading, packing and documentation checks are handled.            |
| `/packaging`                | Packing research                    | bulk pulses packaging, export packaging India                                 | Explain available packing structures and private-label planning.                     |
| `/logistics`                | Shipment planning                   | FOB CIF agricultural exports India                                            | Explain ports, freight modes, documents and routing assumptions.                     |
| `/markets` and market pages | International buyer relevance       | Indian exporter to UAE, Middle East agricultural exporter, supplier to Africa | Provide non-doorway regional guidance and link to detailed export routes.            |

## Internal Linking Model

- Homepage links to products, markets, trust sections and contact.
- Product catalogue links to every product detail page.
- Product detail pages link to related products, region routes and contact.
- Market pages link to detailed `/export-to/[region]` pages and the RFQ form.
- Informational pages link back to `/contact`, `/products` and relevant market/product routes.

## Content Rules

- Do not publish fake certifications, volumes, testimonials, rankings or country coverage.
- Use placeholders or neutral language when product specifications are not confirmed.
- Keep each market page useful; avoid creating thin country pages without distinct buyer information.
